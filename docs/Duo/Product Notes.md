# Duo Product Notes

## Positioning

Duo is about sustainable AI coding, not raw speed.

## Problem

AI can generate too much code for a human to meaningfully review. That creates burnout and distance from the codebase.

## Core Promise

Keep the human involved where understanding matters.

## Modes

- Pair: human and AI collaborate actively.
- Delegate: AI builds, human reviews.
- Review: human builds, AI reviews.

## Differentiator

Understanding checkpoints make comprehension observable instead of assumed.
